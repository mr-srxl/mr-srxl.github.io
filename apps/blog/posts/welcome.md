# Welcome 

This site has been many things in my head — a portfolio, a notebook, a
small operating system that happens to live in a browser. What it's
finally become is closer to a **garden**: something you tend slowly,
visit when you have a moment, and leave better than you found it.

## Why not a normal portfolio

Normal portfolios are resumes with better fonts. They ask you to skim
and move on. I wanted somewhere you could actually *sit* for a minute —
open a window, read a chapter of Python, stare at a wallpaper.

> The quiet study of how to be great, how to be better, how to live well.

That's the mood. Nothing here is in a hurry.

## What lives here

- **About** — who I am, briefly.
- **Path** — the road I've been walking, one step at a time.
- **Skills** — what I actually enjoy working on.
- **Python** — a small course, written the way I'd want to be taught.
- **Notes** — these short pieces.
- **Gallery** — a few quiet places.
- **Terminal** — because everything is nicer with a prompt.

## A small rule for this place

If it doesn't feel calm, it doesn't ship. That's it. Everything else
follows from that.
